## Create reference lessons

Create displays the complete Mouse Wedding and Rooster and Hen photographs independently from the progressive SVG drawings in `drawing-tradition.js`. The latter are new, simplified educational interpretations based on the visible compositions. They are not museum objects, facsimiles, recovered woodblocks, or historical printing sequences. Lettering is omitted instead of invented. Each study has five drawing lessons: composition, silhouettes, supporting forms, color, and contour details. Preset colors are screen approximations.

The earlier 18 generic examples are no longer offered in the picker. Their archived SVG files remain on disk for project history; **Find inspiration** now uses the six shop photographs above. **My own idea** retains a clearly optional arrangement of abstract shapes. User drawings and exported PNGs remain the visitor's own digital work; photographs and study diagrams are not inserted into the drawing canvas.

## Educational illustrations and texture

The village, woodblock, brush, and gallery objects are contemporary illustrations. The process story's inline SVG is a simplified contemporary Mouse Wedding study, with separate red, green, white, yellow, and black groups. It is not a recovered block set or a reproduction of the photographed museum print. The existing workshop studies and simulation are unchanged.

`paper-fibers.svg` is an original, subtle vector texture suggesting paper fibers, not a photograph or scan of historical dó paper. Interface pigment colors are screen approximations.
